# Story Template

## Part 1 — The Beginning

Introduce the main character, establish where and when the story takes place, and show the character’s situation before the main problem begins.

## Part 2 — The Inciting Incident

Introduce an event, discovery, decision, or problem that disrupts the character’s situation and gives the story a clear direction.

## Part 3 — The Goal

Show what the main character wants to accomplish in response to the problem and explain why achieving this goal matters to them.

## Part 4 — The First Challenge

Introduce an obstacle that makes the character’s goal more difficult and show how the character responds to it.

## Part 5 — The Complications

Add new obstacles, consequences, or discoveries that make the situation more difficult and force the character to reconsider their approach.

## Part 6 — The Turning Point

Introduce a major event or revelation that changes the character’s understanding of the situation or significantly alters what happens next.

## Part 7 — The New Plan

Show how the character responds to the turning point by making a new decision, forming a new plan, or changing their approach to the problem.

## Part 8 — The Rising Stakes

Increase the consequences of failure by placing the character in a more difficult situation or forcing them to risk something important.

## Part 9 — The Major Confrontation

Put the character directly against the central problem, opponent, or difficult choice and show what they do when avoiding the conflict is no longer possible.

## Part 10 — The Consequences

Show what happens as a result of the character’s major confrontation and reveal how the outcome changes the character or their situation.

## Part 11 — The Final Choice

Give the character one final decision, action, or realization that determines how the central conflict will ultimately be handled.

## Part 12 — The Ending

Either resolve the main conflict and show what happens afterward, or end with an unanswered question, revelation, or new threat that leaves the story open for what comes next.
