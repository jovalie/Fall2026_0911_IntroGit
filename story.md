# Story Template

## Part 1 — The Beginning

*Introduce the main character, establish where and when the story takes place, and show the character’s situation before the main problem begins.*

## Part 2 — The Inciting Incident

*Introduce an event, discovery, decision, or problem that disrupts the character’s situation and gives the story a clear direction.*

## Part 3 — The Goal

_Show what the main character wants to accomplish in response to the problem and explain why achieving this goal matters to them._

## Part 4 — The First Challenge

_Introduce an obstacle that makes the character’s goal more difficult and show how the character responds to it._

## Part 5 — The Complications

_Add new obstacles, consequences, or discoveries that make the situation more difficult and force the character to reconsider their approach._

## Part 6 — The Turning Point

_Introduce a major event or revelation that changes the character’s understanding of the situation or significantly alters what happens next._

## Part 7 — The New Plan

_Show how the character responds to the turning point by making a new decision, forming a new plan, or changing their approach to the problem._

## Part 8 — The Rising Stakes

_Increase the consequences of failure by placing the character in a more difficult situation or forcing them to risk something important._

## Part 9 — The Major Confrontation

_Put the character directly against the central problem, opponent, or difficult choice and show what they do when avoiding the conflict is no longer possible._

## Part 10 — The Final Choice

_Give the character one final decision, action, or realization that determines how the central conflict will ultimately be handled._

## Part 11 — The Ending

_Either resolve the main conflict and show what happens afterward, or end with an unanswered question, revelation, or new threat that leaves the story open for what comes next._
